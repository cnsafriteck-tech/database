Why Recruitment Credits Matter

Recruitment credits help Afriteck measure and recognise each reseller's contribution to the growth of the company. When the company has staff vacancies or opportunities to recruit new team members, we will review reseller credit performance to help identify potential candidates for employment.

Your recruitment performance helps us recognise commitment, consistency and contribution to the company. Higher credit performance may strengthen your consideration for future employment opportunities.

Important: Recruitment credits do not guarantee employment. All appointments depend on company requirements, available vacancies and selection criteria.
